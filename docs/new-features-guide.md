# Team users (add / manage logins)

Where: Sidebar > SETUP > Users & Roles (Company Admin and Super Admin only).
Click "+ Add User", enter name, email (used to log in), role and a password of at least 8 characters, then Save. Role "Staff" can use billing, customers and stock view; role "Admin" has full access to the business except SaaS Admin. Use Edit to rename, change role or reset the password. Use Deactivate to stop a user logging in without deleting them. The business owner and your own account cannot be deactivated or have their role changed. Users are created only for the current business; Super Admin accounts cannot be created here.

# Payment reminders (WhatsApp)

Where: Sidebar > SALES > Payment Reminders.
Shows every customer who owes money, with total due, overdue amount and the oldest overdue days. Invoices without a due date count as due on the invoice date. Click "WhatsApp reminder" next to a customer: WhatsApp opens with a ready message listing the pending invoices; press Send. The customer needs a valid 10-digit Indian mobile or WhatsApp number on the Customers screen. Use the search box and the "Overdue only" tick to filter.

# GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

Where: Sidebar > ACCOUNTING > GST Returns (or GST > returns tab).
Pick the return month. The screen shows GSTR-1 counts (B2B, B2C large, B2C small, credit notes) and the GSTR-3B table (outward tax, credit notes, input tax credit, net payable). Download "Excel (GSTR-1 + 3B)" for sheets B2B, B2CL, B2CS, Credit Notes, HSN Summary and GSTR3B, or "GSTR-1 JSON" for the GST offline tool. Figures come from approved sales invoices, approved credit notes (sales returns) and approved purchase bills. Draft and cancelled documents are excluded. A warning is shown for items without HSN and for non-standard GST rates. These are working figures: always verify in the GST portal or offline tool before filing. E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill generation are not available yet.

# Global search

Type at least 2 characters in the top-bar search box to see matching invoices, customers and products. Click a result to open it. Results depend on the user's role.
